# Official IELTS Writing Assessment Framework & Band Descriptors

This document details the four official IELTS Writing criteria, focusing specifically on the distinctions separating **Band 7**, **Band 8**, and **Band 9**.

---

## 1. Task Response (Task 2) & Task Achievement (Task 1)

### Band 7
- **Task 2**: Covers all parts of the task. Presents a clear position throughout. Presents, extends, and supports main ideas, but there may be a tendency to overgeneralize and/or supporting ideas may lack focus or depth.
- **Task 1**: Covers the requirements of the task. Presents a clear overview of main trends, differences, or stages. Clearly presents and highlights key features/bullet points, but could be more fully extended.

### Band 8 (Target)
- **Task 2**: Sufficiently addresses **all parts** of the task. Presents a **well-developed response** to the question with relevant, extended, and supported ideas. Arguments are sustained and logically reasoned; generalizations are avoided.
- **Task 1**: Covers all requirements of the task sufficiently. Presents a **clear, comprehensive overview** of key trends, differences, or stages. Highlights all key features and illustrates them fully and appropriately without superfluous data dumping.

### Band 9
- **Task 2**: Fully addresses all parts of the task. Presents a fully developed position in answer to the question with fully extended and well-supported ideas.
- **Task 1**: Fully satisfies all requirements. Skillfully integrates relevant details with comprehensive and insightful overviews.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> **Band 7 vs Band 8 Leap**: At Band 7, arguments are often stated and given a brief example, but the *explanatory mechanism* is missing. At Band 8, every argument is followed through its causal logic (Claim -> Reason -> Explanation -> Impact).

## 2. Coherence and Cohesion (CC)

### Band 7
- Logically organizes information and ideas; there is clear progression throughout.
- Uses a range of cohesive devices appropriately, though there may be some under-/over-use.
- Presents a clear central topic within each paragraph.

### Band 8 (Target)
- Sequences information and ideas **logically and seamlessly**.
- Manages all aspects of cohesion well. Uses cohesive devices **naturally and unobtrusively** (avoids mechanical overuse of words like *"Furthermore"*, *"Moreover"*, *"In addition"*).
- Uses paragraphing sufficiently and appropriately, with impeccable internal topic progression.

### Band 9
- Uses cohesion in such a way that it attracts no attention.
- Skillfully manages paragraphing with effortless logical flow and thematic unity.

> [!TIP]
> **Examiner Insight on Cohesion**: Band 7 candidates often start every sentence with a linking word followed by a comma (*"Furthermore,..."*, *"Moreover,..."*, *"In contrast,..."*). At Band 8, cohesion is achieved **referentially**—using determiners, pronouns, nominalization, and topical bridges rather than obvious signposts.

## 3. Lexical Resource (LR)

### Band 7
- Uses a sufficient range of vocabulary to allow some flexibility and precision.
- Uses less common lexical items with some awareness of style and collocation.
- May produce occasional errors in word choice, spelling, and/or word formation.

### Band 8 (Target)
- Uses a **wide, flexible vocabulary** to convey precise meanings fluently.
- **Skillfully uses uncommon lexical items**, with natural awareness of collocation and register.
- Errors in word choice and collocation are **rare and minor** (slips rather than systematic gaps).
- Rare spelling or word formation errors.

### Band 9
- Uses a wide vocabulary with very natural and sophisticated control of lexical features; rare minor slips characteristic of native speaker writing.

> [!WARNING]
> **The "Sophistication" Trap**: Using rare or fancy words in the wrong context (e.g., *"This policy will ameliorate the deleterious conundrum"*) will drop your score to Band 6. Band 8 vocabulary is defined by **precision and natural collocations**, not decorative complexity.

---

## 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)

### Band 7
- Uses a variety of complex structures.
- Produces frequent error-free sentences.
- Has good control of grammar and punctuation but may make a few systematic or noticeable errors.

### Band 8 (Target)
- Uses a **wide range of structures flexibly and accurately**.
- **The majority of sentences are completely error-free**.
- Makes only very occasional or non-systematic errors; minor inappropriacies do not impede communication.
- Punctuation is consistently well-managed.

### Band 9
- Uses a full range of structures with full flexibility and accuracy; rare minor slips.

## Summary Comparison Matrix

| Criterion | Band 7 Ceiling | Band 8 Requirement | Band 9 Mastery |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **TR / TA** | Ideas listed and supported, but depth can be uneven or generalized. | Every core claim fully extended through causal reasoning and impact. | Effortless argumentative nuance and flawless completeness. |
| **CC** | Mechanical linking words used noticeably at the start of sentences. | Cohesion is fluid, referential, and largely invisible. | Complete organic harmony across the entire composition. |
| **LR** | Good range with occasional awkward collocations or forced words. | Precise, idiomatic collocations; rare minor lexical slips. | Native-level precision, register, and natural flexibility. |
| **GRA** | Frequent error-free sentences, but noticeable occasional grammar slips. | The vast majority of sentences are error-free; flexible complex syntax. | Impeccable grammatical command with full structural dexterity. |
