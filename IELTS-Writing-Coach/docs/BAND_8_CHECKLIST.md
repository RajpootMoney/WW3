# IELTS Band 8 Pre-Submission Checklist

Use this 15-point verification checklist before finalizing any Task 1 or Task 2 essay.

---

## Task 2: Band 8 Verification Checklist

### Criterion 1: Task Response (TR)
- [ ] **1. Complete Prompt Coverage**: Have you answered every sub-question and addressed every micro-topic within the prompt?
- [ ] **2. Unwavering Position**: Is your thesis statement clearly stated in the introduction, maintained throughout both body paragraphs, and reaffirmed in the conclusion?
- [ ] **3. C-R-E-I Depth**: Does every main idea follow through Claim → Reason → Explanation → Impact without circular reasoning or unextended bullet points?
- [ ] **4. Academic Balance**: If writing a discussion or concession essay, is the counter-view treated objectively before being logically refuted?

### Criterion 2: Coherence and Cohesion (CC)
- [ ] **5. Organic Progression**: Does each paragraph present one clear central idea expressed in a focused topic sentence?
- [ ] **6. Referential Cohesion**: Have you avoided starting consecutive sentences with *"Furthermore"*, *"Moreover"*, or *"In addition"*? Are ideas linked using demonstrative pronouns (*"This trend"*, *"Such policies"*) and lexical bridges?
- [ ] **7. Paragraph Balance**: Are your two body paragraphs roughly equal in length, depth, and developmental rigor (approx. 90–110 words each)?

### Criterion 3: Lexical Resource (LR)
- [ ] **8. Natural Collocations**: Are verbs and adjectives naturally paired with their nouns (*"curb emissions"*, *"foster innovation"*, *"widen disparities"*)?
- [ ] **9. Precise Academic Register**: Have you avoided conversational idioms (*"at the end of the day"*, *"in a nutshell"*, *"kids"*, *"lots of"*) and avoided memorized "fancy" words that distort meaning?
- [ ] **10. Controlled Paraphrasing**: Are prompt keywords paraphrased accurately without changing their legal or thematic scope?

### Criterion 4: Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA)
- [ ] **11. Sentence Variety**: Does the essay contain a deliberate combination of compound sentences, concessive clauses (*although / despite*), conditionals (*were governments to...*), and participle modifiers (*resulting in...*)?
- [ ] **12. High Error-Free Majority**: Are at least 70–80% of sentences completely free of grammatical or punctuation slips?
- [ ] **13. Comma Splice & Boundary Scan**: Are independent clauses properly separated by coordinating conjunctions, semicolons, or full stops?

### Operational & Exam Conditions
- [ ] **14. Word Count Target**: Is Task 2 comfortably between 265 and 290 words (avoiding both under-length penalties and verbose time-wasting)?
- [ ] **15. S-V-A-P-T Final Sweep**: Has a dedicated 3-to-4-minute proofreading pass checked Subject-Verb agreement, Articles, Prepositions, and Tenses?

## Task 1: Band 8 Verification Checklist (Quick Reference)

- [ ] **Clear, Uncluttered Overview**: Did you clearly state the 2–3 overarching macro trends, major contrasts, or core process stages without quoting specific raw numbers?
- [ ] **Key Features Illustrated**: Are the main categories accurately grouped and supported by selected key data points?
- [ ] **No Speculation**: Did you avoid interpreting reasons for the data that are not presented in the visual prompt?
- [ ] **Word Count**: Is the report comfortably between 165 and 190 words?
