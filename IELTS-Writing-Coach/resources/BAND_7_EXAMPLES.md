# Benchmark Resources: Band 7 Essay Analysis

This document provides an authentic sample of Band 7 writing accompanied by detailed examiner commentary analyzing the exact ceilings that prevent it from reaching Band 8.

---

## Prompt (Task 2 - Technology & Society)
> *Some people believe that technological developments lead to the loss of traditional culture. Others, however, argue that technology helps to preserve and promote traditional culture.*
> *Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

---

## Band 7 Sample Essay

It is often argued that modern technology is destroying traditional cultures, while others believe that it actually assists in protecting and spreading cultural heritage. In my opinion, although technology can sometimes cause young people to forget their traditions, on the whole, it provides powerful tools to document and preserve culture for future generations.

On the one hand, critics argue that globalization fueled by technology has led to the erosion of local customs. The widespread availability of smartphones and internet access has exposed younger generations to Western media, music, and fashion. As a result, many traditional practices, such as folk dancing and indigenous craftsmanship, are losing their appeal. Furthermore, international fast-food chains and entertainment platforms are replacing local cultural gatherings. Consequently, some traditional values and languages are in danger of disappearing because the youth prefer modern digital lifestyle.

On the other hand, proponents argue that technology plays an essential role in keeping traditional culture alive. Digital archives, high-definition photography, and video recording allow historians to record endangered languages, ancient rituals, and historical monuments. For example, museums now use virtual reality tours so people worldwide can experience historical sites without traveling. In addition, social media platforms allow traditional artisans to sell their crafts globally, which helps their local economy and keeps their art relevant today.

In conclusion, while technology presents some risks to traditional customs by promoting Western culture among the youth, I believe it is ultimately a valuable asset. If used properly, technology allows ancient traditions to be recorded, shared, and preserved more effectively than ever before.

## Detailed Examiner Analysis: Why This is Band 7 (Not Band 8)

### 1. Task Response (TR): Band 7
- **Examiner Note**: The candidate addresses both views and presents a clear opinion throughout. However, some ideas lack depth. For instance, in body paragraph 1, the essay mentions that *"traditional values and languages are in danger of disappearing"*, but does not explain the exact social mechanism of how digital lifestyle replaces language transmission. It relies on a list of points rather than deep causal expansion.

### 2. Coherence & Cohesion (CC): Band 7
- **Examiner Note**: Paragraphing is logical, but linking devices are **mechanical and formulaic**. Notice the repetitive sentence starters: *"On the one hand"*, *"As a result"*, *"Furthermore"*, *"Consequently"*, *"On the other hand"*, *"In addition"*. While accurate, this attracts attention to itself, representing a classic Band 7 ceiling. Band 8 requires referential, natural linking.

### 3. Lexical Resource (LR): Band 7
- **Examiner Note**: Vocabulary is sufficient and generally appropriate (*"erosion of local customs"*, *"indigenous craftsmanship"*, *"digital archives"*). However, there are vague colloquial phrases: *"modern digital lifestyle"*, *"people worldwide"*, *"valuable asset"*, *"if used properly"*. It lacks the sustained precision and sophisticated collocations required for Band 8.

### 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA): Band 7
- **Examiner Note**: The candidate constructs good complex sentences with high accuracy (*"In my opinion, although technology can sometimes cause... it provides..."*). However, the range is somewhat repetitive (mostly *as a result*, *because*, *while*). Advanced structures like inversions, cleft sentences, or participle clauses are absent.
