## Prompt (Task 2 - Technology & Society)
> *Some people believe that technological developments lead to the loss of traditional culture. Others, however, argue that technology helps to preserve and promote traditional culture.*
> *Discuss both views and give your own opinion.*

---

## Band 8 Model Essay

The accelerated pace of technological innovation has sparked an intense debate regarding its implications for cultural heritage. While critics assert that digital connectivity accelerates cultural homogenization, proponents contend that modern technology serves as an indispensable instrument for cultural preservation. This essay argues that although uncurated digital media poses legitimate threats to indigenous customs, technological applications ultimately offer unprecedented capabilities to document, revitalize, and disseminate traditional practices globally.

Opponents of technological ubiquity emphasize its corrosive impact on localized customs. The rapid expansion of borderless digital platforms frequently exposes youth to dominant global consumer culture, thereby supplanting ancestral rituals with imported Western habits. When adolescent generations prioritize globalized social media trends over vernacular storytelling or localized festivities, the intergenerational transmission of intangible heritage is severely ruptured. Furthermore, the algorithmic curation of mass entertainment tends to prioritize sensational, commercially lucrative content, leaving regional folk traditions marginalized and economically unviable for traditional practitioners.

Conversely, technological innovations provide conservationists and cultural practitioners with unprecedented preservation mechanisms. Advanced digital archiving, high-fidelity spatial scanning, and acoustic mapping now enable vulnerable historical sites and endangered languages to be permanently safeguarded against physical degradation or linguistic extinction. Moreover, decentralized multimedia networks allow marginalized artisans to bypass domestic gatekeepers, cultivating global audiences for their craft. It is precisely through this international digital visibility that indigenous art forms secure the economic viability necessary for long-term survival in an interconnected economy.

In conclusion, while the pervasive influx of commercialized media undeniably imperils localized cultural expressions, technological instruments remain vital safeguards against cultural oblivion. Provided that societies leverage digital platforms intentionally to archive and celebrate indigenous traditions, technology will act as a potent protector rather than a destroyer of ancestral heritage.

## Detailed Examiner Commentary: The Band 8 Distinction

### 1. Task Response (TR): Band 8
- **Full Coverage & Nuance**: Both views are explored with substantial causal depth. Body Paragraph 1 does not merely list points; it explains the mechanism of how digital trends break the intergenerational transmission of heritage.
- **Sustained Position**: The candidate's stance is declared unambiguously in the introduction (*"...technological applications ultimately offer unprecedented capabilities..."*), logically reinforced through the body paragraphs, and reaffirmed in the conclusion.

### 2. Coherence & Cohesion (CC): Band 8
- **Invisible, Referential Linking**: Notice how sentences connect without relying exclusively on *"Furthermore"* or *"Moreover"*:
  - *"When adolescent generations prioritize..."* links back to the exposed youth.
  - *"It is precisely through this international digital visibility that..."* refers back to the decentralized multimedia networks.
- **Flawless Topic Progression**: Each paragraph develops one central thesis that unfolds logically sentence by sentence.

### 3. Lexical Resource (LR): Band 8
- **Precise, Natural Academic Collocations**:
  - `Cultural homogenization`, `indigenous customs`, `corrosive impact`, `intergenerational transmission`, `intangible heritage`, `high-fidelity spatial scanning`, `cultural oblivion`.
- **Zero "Thesaurus Distortion"**: Every lexical choice fits its syntactic and academic register perfectly; there are no forced or archaic vocabulary words.

### 4. Grammatical Range and Accuracy (GRA): Band 8
- **Structural Dexterity**:
  - Concession clause: *"While critics assert that... proponents contend..."*
  - Participle modifier: *"...thereby supplanting ancestral rituals..."*, *"...leaving regional folk traditions marginalized..."*
  - Cleft focus structure: *"It is precisely through this international digital visibility that indigenous art forms secure..."*
  - Condition: *"Provided that societies leverage digital platforms intentionally..."*
- **Majority Error-Free Sentences**: Impeccable punctuation, zero comma splices, consistent tenses, and natural syntax.
