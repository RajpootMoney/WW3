## 3. High-Priority Prepositional Collocation Triad (Causation)

Mastered causation markers essential for Task 2 Impact sentences:

```
1. [Factor] + leads to + [Noun / Outcome]
   Example: "Digital literacy leads to career acceleration."

2. [Factor] + contributes to + [Noun / Outcome]
   Example: "Physical wellness contributes to mental resilience."

3. [Factor] + results in + [Noun / Outcome]
   Example: "Rigorous research results in breakthrough discoveries."
```
